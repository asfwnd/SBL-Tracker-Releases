Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no premium tier. The APK is a straight download.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Every set logs instantly on-device and syncs to the cloud the next time you're connected.

Is there an iOS app?

Not yet. Use the web app in Safari and add it to your home screen for an app-like experience.

Can I export my data?

Your data is yours. Export or delete it whenever you want, no questions asked.

What devices are supported?

Android 8.0 and up for the native app. Any modern browser for the web app, on PC or iOS.
